Projects Guardian: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Projects Guardian is a personal tool that runs on the computer of its owner, IbnKabed. It is not offered to the public.

Google Drive access

  • It uploads weekly backup copies of the owner's own projects to the owner's own Google Drive.
  • It uses the drive.file permission, so it can reach only the files it created itself, and nothing else in the Drive.

Data use and sharing

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or for training AI models.
  • The tool collects no data from any other person.

Stopping access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

بالعربية

Projects Guardian أداة شخصية تعمل على جهاز صاحبها، وليست خدمة عامة. ترفع كل أسبوع نسخًا من مشاريعه إلى Google Drive الخاص به، ولا تصل إلا إلى الملفات التي أنشأتها بنفسها. ولا تبيع أي بيانات ولا تشاركها مع أحد، ولا تجمع بيانات أي شخص آخر.
