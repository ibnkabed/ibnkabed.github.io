Sihha: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Sihha is a personal health record tool that runs on the computer of its developer and only user, ALSHAMMARY, ABDULLAH METIB A (IbnKabed), the individual developer registered on HUAWEI Developers. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

Data read from Huawei Health

With the user's own authorization through his HUAWEI ID, Sihha reads, and never writes, the following data from Huawei Health through Health Service Kit:

  • Steps, distance, calories, and moderate-to-high-intensity minutes.
  • Exercise records (type, start and end time, duration, distance, and workout heart rate).
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate.
  • Sleep records and sleep stages.

How the data is used

  • The data is added to the user's own daily activity and sleep record, so he can follow his own trends and prepare a summary for his doctor.
  • It is not used for diagnosis, treatment, advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

Storage, sharing and transfer

  • The data is stored only on the user's own computer, in Saudi Arabia. It is not uploaded to any server owned by the developer.
  • It is not sold, shared with any third party, or transferred to any other country.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer and are never published.
  • Sihha collects no data from any other person.

Stopping access and deleting data

The user can withdraw Sihha's access at any time from his HUAWEI ID authorization settings or from the Huawei Health app. Withdrawing access stops all reading, and the stored copy can be deleted from the computer at any time.
